Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SweCS2 keeps data collection to a minimum. Here is what happens when you use the site.

Local storage and caching

Match, team, and player data is cached in your browser's local storage so pages load quickly and stay usable when the API is briefly unreachable. This cache never leaves your device and is not linked to any personal identifier.

Bot protection

The site uses Cloudflare Turnstile to verify that requests to the backend come from a real browser rather than automated scraping. Turnstile runs invisibly and follows Cloudflare's own privacy practices.

Third-party data

Match and roster data is sourced from PandaScore, GRID, and Valve's public regional standings repository. No account information, IP addresses, or browsing activity is shared with these providers beyond what's needed to fetch match data.

No tracking, no ads

This site does not run analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and does not sell or share any data about visitors.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@rskcs2.github.io.
